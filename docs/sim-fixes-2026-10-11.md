# TownShape fixes for the social sim (2026-10-11)

Answers items 10 and 11 of `social-sim-demo/docs/townshape-notes-2026-10-11.md`
(social-sim commit 474708b). Both fixes are on TownShape `main`, and the full
TownShape test suite passes (512 tests).

| Item | Commit | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 10: every placed building adds a field district | `669ee7f` | `place_building` no longer adds new fields |
| 11: the river mouth is drawn as a land band | `d102f68` | the coastline has no holes, and the water polygons are merged |

## Item 10: placed buildings no longer add field districts

- When `place_building` finds no gap inside the walls, it falls back to
  `add_buildings(..., resow=False)` (`town_db/construction.py`).
- Fields the new building lands on are still trimmed or converted to
  housing. You'll see this in `trimmed_district_ids`, `converted_district_ids`
  and `living_district_ids` in `construction_edits`. No new fields are added:
  `new_field_district_ids` is always `[]` for `place_building` edits.
- Calling `add_buildings` directly still adds new fields by default
  (`resow=True`).
- New farmland is now left to the sim's own farms (farmsteads item,
  integration doc 3.3).

## Item 11: the river mouth is now drawn as water

There were two causes, both present at generation (year 0):

1. **A gap in the coastline shape** (`town_shaper/water.py`). The sea
   polygon is a curved strip joined to an open-sea box. Where the strip
   curved away from the box, it left a thin hole of land inside the sea,
   about 6×66 units at the river mouth. That hole was the road-like band.
   Coastlines now have no holes.
2. **River/sea overlap under even-odd** (`settlemaker_bridge/pipeline.py`).
   The water polygons are now merged into one shape before they go to
   settlemaker. Settlemaker applies even-odd both to draw `#water` and to
   decide which areas are water, so this also fixes how the river mouth is
   classified. It isn't only a drawing fix.

Both fixes are needed. With only the first one, the seed-7 town still had a
167-unit river/sea overlap.

Result for `TownParameters(seed=7, target_population=1500, num_rivers=1,
has_coastline=True, has_port=True, has_citadel=True)`: the `#water` fill
path went from 3 overlapping subpaths to 1.

## For the re-run

- **Regenerate the town.** The water is drawn into the map at generation, so
  towns generated before `d102f68` still have the band.
- **Expect some layout shift on coastal seeds.** The river mouth and the
  sliver used to count as land and now count as water, so a coastal town on
  the same seed can lay out a little differently than before.
- **Checks:**
  - every `place_building` row in `construction_edits` has
    `"new_field_district_ids": []`;
  - the fill path in `<g id="water">` has one subpath per separate body of
    water, with no overlaps.

## Not investigated yet

In a small test town, `place_building` returned `None` (no room) when
`preferred_district_id` was a farmland district. If the sim ever passes a
farm district as the preferred district, report it back and TownShape will
look into it.
